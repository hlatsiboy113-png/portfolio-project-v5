About Me

THE STORY BEHIND THE CODE

I am an emerging frontend developer from Soweto. My path runs through a creative background in advertising, photography, videography and music, then professional experience that taught me reliability and collaboration, into a deliberate redirection toward software.

I build interfaces and systems with the same care I once brought to creative work: structure, clarity and respect for the people who use them.

I am not a finished product. I am becoming — shipping, learning and refining how I think.
